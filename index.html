Format Sheet

2026 DBI Official Rules & Scoring Reference

Round 1Wild Horse · Mon AM

Modified Stableford (Individual) — Quota = 38 – Handicap. Points: Bogey=1, Par=2, Birdie=4, Eagle=8. Best score vs quota wins.

1st→14 pts
2nd→13
3rd→12
↓ -1 per place
Round 2Wild Horse · Mon PM

4-Person Scramble — Texas Scramble Rules (3 drives each). Low team score wins.

1st→7 pts
2nd→5
3rd→4
4th→2
5th→0
Round 3Bayside · Tue AM

2v2 Best Ball Match Play — Nassau. Lowest individual net score between teams wins each hole.

Front 9 Win→3 pts
Back 9 Win→3
Total Win→3
Winning team bonus→3
Round 4Bayside · Tue PM

4-Person Shamble (80% HDCP) — Best drive selected, all play own ball. Team score = sum of 2 best individual net scores.

1st→10 pts
2nd→7
3rd→5
4th→3
5th→0
Round 5Wild Horse · Wed AM

Individual Net Score — Gross – Handicap = Net Score. Best net wins.

1st→16 pts
2nd→15
3rd→14
↓ -1 per place
Round 6Wild Horse · Wed PM

2v2 Scramble Match Play — Nassau. A-Player uses 70% HDCP · B-Player uses 30% HDCP. Lowest 2-person scramble net score wins each hole.

Front 9 Win→3 pts
Back 9 Win→3
Total Win→3
Winning team bonus→3
🎯 ShootoutWed 7pm

Top 10 qualifiers. Entry by seed: 7–10=Hole 1 · 5–6=Hole 2 · 3–4=Hole 3 · 2=Hole 4 · 1=Hole 5. Holes 1–3: 2 eliminated. Holes 4–7: 1 eliminated. Ties → putt-off. Mulligans: HDCP 20–23=1 · 24+=2. Cannot use on putting surface — if hit, must be taken.
