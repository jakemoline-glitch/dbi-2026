2026 DBI Official Rules & Scoring Reference

Round 1Wild Horse · Mon AM

Modified Stableford (Individual) — Quota = 38 – Handicap. Points: Bogey=1, Par=2, Birdie=4, Eagle=8. Best score vs quota wins.

1st→14 pts
2nd→13
3rd→12
↓ -1 per place
Round 2Wild Horse · Mon PM

4-Person Scramble — Texas Scramble Rules (3 drives each). Low team score wins.

1st→7 pts
2nd→5
3rd→4
4th→2
5th→0
Round 3Bayside · Tue AM

2v2 Best Ball Match Play — Nassau. Lowest individual net score between teams wins each hole.

Front 9 Win→3 pts
Back 9 Win→3
Total Win→3
Winning team bonus→3
Round 4Bayside · Tue PM

4-Person Shamble (80% HDCP) — Best drive selected, all play own ball. Team score = sum of 2 best individual net scores.

1st→10 pts
2nd→7
3rd→5
4th→3
5th→0
Round 5Wild Horse · Wed AM

Individual Net Score — Gross – Handicap = Net Score. Best net wins.

1st→16 pts
2nd→15
3rd→14
↓ -1 per place
Round 6Wild Horse · Wed PM

2v2 Scramble Match Play — Nassau. One ball, one score per hole per team. Combined team HDCP = A-Player (70%) + B-Player (30%). Lower combined HDCP team gives strokes to the higher combined HDCP team, allocated by hole difficulty. Lower net score on each hole wins.

Front 9 Win→3 pts
Back 9 Win→3
Total Win→3
Winning team bonus→3

Ties & Putt-Offs

Any tie at the elimination cut line on a given hole results in a putt-off — tied players putt from the same distance until one makes it and one doesn't. The player who misses is eliminated. If multiple players are tied for the last elimination spot, all tied players participate in the putt-off.
